TinyPrint printer setup

The register cannot pair Bluetooth directly with TinyPrint from a website. Connect the printer in the TinyPrint app first.

  1. Switch on the blue Gabba Goods printer and make sure it has paper.
  2. Turn Bluetooth on on the iPad or Android tablet.
  3. Open TinyPrint and go to its printer/device connection screen.
  4. Select your printer and wait until TinyPrint shows it as connected.
  5. Return to this register. When a customer wants a receipt, choose Send to TinyPrint and select TinyPrint in the share menu if it is offered.
Connection status is confirmed inside TinyPrint. This register does not claim the printer is connected automatically.
